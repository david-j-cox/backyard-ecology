        All sites across study phases

        Daily feeder visits from each site's daily summaries, one row per site, shaded by that site's own phases. Momentum fits are drawn on each extinction phase.

        Behavioral momentum

        How fast visits fall off after food is removed, fitted for every extinction phase: B(t) = B₀e−bt and B(t) = B₀e−bt + c, where t is days since the extinction began. Higher b means faster extinction. Same method as the study: zero-visit days are left out of the fit.
